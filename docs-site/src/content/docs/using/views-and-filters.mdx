Every [view](/using/navigation/#views) can be shaped to the task at hand: as a list or a board, grouped
by status or priority, filtered down to one label, or searched for a word in the title. The three buttons
at the top right of a view hold these controls: board/list, **Filter** and **Display**.

## List and board

Press <Kbd keys="mod+b" /> or click the board button to switch a view between list and board. The board
has one column per status. Across several teams, statuses with the same name and kind share a column.
Done and canceled columns start collapsed; click a column heading to expand or collapse it.

<Screenshot name="board" alt="The Active view as a board, with columns Todo, In Progress and In Review and issue cards in each" />

The board is available in All issues, Active, Backlog, Sprint and project views. Triage has no board,
and the All issues board leaves out issues in Triage.

To move an issue to another status on the board:

- Drag the card into another column, or
- Press <Kbd keys="shift+arrowleft" /> or <Kbd keys="shift+arrowright" /> to move the focused card (or all
  selected cards) one column to the left or right. This also works into a collapsed column.

<Kbd keys="arrowleft" /> and <Kbd keys="arrowright" /> move the focus between columns,
<Kbd keys="j" /> and <Kbd keys="k" /> within a column. If a team's workflow does not allow a move, the
column is marked while you drag and Selfbound tells you why.

## Display options

Press <Kbd keys="shift+v" /> or click **Display** to open the display options:

- **Grouping**: by Status, Team, Priority, Project, Sprint, or None. The sprint view also offers
  **Planned / added later**. The available choices depend on the view.
- **Ordering**: by Priority, Manual, Last updated, Created, Due date, and in some views Status.
- **Show completed**: in views where done and canceled issues are relevant (All issues, Sprint,
  projects), this switch shows or hides them.
- **Properties**: which details each row shows, such as ID, status, labels, project, estimate or due
  date. Click a property to toggle it.
- **Restore defaults** resets everything to the view's original setup.

A dot on the **Display** button means the view differs from its defaults. Display options are saved per
view and per team in this browser, so the Backlog of one team can look different from another's.

### Collapsing groups

Click a group heading, or press <Kbd keys="t" /> to collapse or expand the group of the focused issue.
Collapsed groups stay collapsed the next time you open the view.

### Manual order

With the ordering set to **Manual** and the grouping to **None**, you decide the order yourself. Press
<Kbd keys="alt+arrowup" /> or <Kbd keys="alt+arrowdown" /> to move the focused issue up or down. The order is
saved on the server, so a backlog sorted this way stays sorted on all your devices. The Backlog view uses
manual order by default.

## Filters

Press <Kbd keys="f" /> or click **Filter** to filter a view.

<Screenshot name="filter" alt="The filter menu over an issue list, with the fields Status, Priority, Team, Label, Project and Sprint, and the Unread (in inbox) checkbox" width={720} />

1. Pick a field: **Status**, **Priority**, **Team**, **Label**, **Project** or **Sprint**.
2. Check one or more values. The menu stays open, so you can check several.
3. Press <Kbd keys="escape" /> to go back to the fields, and again to close.

You can also skip the first step: type right away, for example "urgent" or "bug", and the menu lists
matching values of all fields at once.

How filters combine: several values of one field mean "any of these" (Priority Urgent or High). Several
fields mean "all of these" (Urgent or High, and label Bug). Project and Sprint also offer "No project" and
"No sprint". **Unread (in inbox)** shows only inbox entries you have not opened yet.

Active filters appear as chips below the header. Click the × on a chip to remove one, or **Reset
filters** to remove all. **Clear filters** in the command menu does the same.

Filtering by a status also shows done and canceled issues of that status, even when **Show completed**
is off.

### Search in this view

Press <Kbd keys="mod+f" /> to filter the current view by text. A search field opens below the header;
type words from the title or an ID. Every word must appear for an issue to stay.

- <Kbd keys="enter" /> or <Kbd keys="arrowdown" /> jumps to the list and keeps the text, so you can work
  with the remaining issues.
- <Kbd keys="escape" /> clears the text and closes the field.

To search all issues, including descriptions and comments, use the issue search with
<Kbd keys="/" /> instead (see [Command menu and search](/using/command-menu/#searching-issues)).

## Sharing a view

Filters, the text search, grouping, ordering and the board layout are part of the page address. Copy it
with the link button at the top right of the view, bookmark it, or send it to yourself on another
device. Opening the link shows the view with the same filters, search, grouping, ordering and layout.
**Show completed** and the visible properties are not part of the address; they come from the display
options saved in the browser that opens the link.

## Peek

Peek shows an issue without leaving the list. Press <Kbd keys="space" /> on a focused issue and a card
with its properties and the start of its description appears at the top right.

<Screenshot name="peek" alt="An issue list with the peek card open at the top right, showing the focused issue's status, priority, estimate, labels and the start of its description" />

- While peek is open, <Kbd keys="j" /> and <Kbd keys="k" /> move through the list and the card follows.
- Press <Kbd keys="space" /> again or <Kbd keys="escape" /> to close it.
- **Hold** <Kbd keys="space" /> for a quick look: the card closes as soon as you let go.

Moving through results in the command menu or the issue search shows the same preview next to the
results.

<Aside type="tip">
Opening an issue from peek is one key away: <Kbd keys="enter" /> opens the focused issue as a full page.
</Aside>
