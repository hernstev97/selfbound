A link to a YouTube video or an X (Twitter) post **alone on its line** is shown as a card instead of a bare
link. This works in issue descriptions, comments and knowledge pages. The text stays plain Markdown: the
card is only how it is displayed, and agents see the link.

## What becomes a card

| Link | Card |
|---|---|
| `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…`, `https://youtu.be/…`, `/shorts/…`, `/live/…`, `/embed/…` | Thumbnail, title and channel. A click on the thumbnail starts the player. A start time in the link (`?t=1m30s`) is kept. |
| `https://x.com/<name>/status/<id>`, `https://twitter.com/…` | Author, text and date of the post, with links to the post and the author on X. No pictures: X's official interface does not provide them. |

The link can stand alone (`https://youtu.be/…`), in angle brackets (`<https://…>`) or as `[any text](https://…)`.
In the last form the text is what you see while no card is shown. A link inside a sentence stays a link.

If the video is private, deleted or cannot be embedded, or the post is gone, or the server cannot reach the
provider right now, the link stays a normal link. Nothing breaks.

## What loads, and when

Selfbound is built so that **your browser does not talk to YouTube or X while you read**.

| When | What happens | Who talks to whom |
|---|---|---|
| A card comes into view | Your browser asks your Selfbound backend for the card data. If it has none yet (or it is old), the backend fetches the title and thumbnail from YouTube, or the author, text and date from X, and keeps a copy. | Browser ↔ your backend. Backend → YouTube / X (`www.youtube.com`, `i.ytimg.com`, `publish.x.com`). |
| The card is shown | The video thumbnail comes from your own backend (`<site>/embed/…`). No script, font or tracker from the provider. | Browser ↔ your backend only. |
| You click the play button of a YouTube card | The player loads from `www.youtube-nocookie.com` (YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode). Only now does your browser contact YouTube, and YouTube sees your address and browser as with any video. | Browser → YouTube. |
| You click a link on a card (author, date, “Open on X”) | A normal link opens in a new tab. | Browser → X / YouTube. |

So reading a description with an X post or a YouTube video sends nothing to those services from your
browser. The backend request carries no cookies and no information about you; it only names the video or
post.

<Aside type="note">
The first request to a provider comes from your backend, so the provider sees the server's address, not
yours. On a self-hosted instance that is your Convex deployment.
</Aside>

## What the backend keeps

- The copy lives in the table `embeds` and in file storage: title, author, post text and date, and a
  video's thumbnail. It is public content of those services.
- YouTube data is refreshed when you view it and it is older than 7 days, X posts after 1 day. A post that was
  deleted later disappears from the card at the next refresh.
- A post deleted on X stays in the copy until the card is shown again after that day, and at most 30 days if nobody shows it.
- A copy that nobody viewed for 30 days is deleted together with its pictures by a nightly job.
- The pictures are served at `<site>/embed/<provider>/<id>/<slot>` without sign-in (like image addresses and
  team logos), because an `<img>` cannot send a token. Only pictures of cached cards are served, and only
  raster images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF) that passed a check when stored.

## Limits

- Only YouTube and X are supported so far. Other providers (Vimeo, Spotify, …) can be added later.
- X cards show author, text and date, but no pictures, videos, replies or quoted posts: Selfbound uses only X's
  official oEmbed interface, which does not provide them. If it stops working, links stay links.
- Pull request texts from GitHub are not changed.
