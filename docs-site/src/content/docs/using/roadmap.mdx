A team can show its work as a **roadmap**: the projects are the stages, in their order, and inside each stage
the issues are sorted into categories with the stage they are in. The **progress** view puts the same stages on
a time axis. Both are made from the issues that are already in Selfbound; nothing is entered twice. For sharing,
the roadmap and the progress view export a picture that contains **public issues only**.

Everything stays behind your sign-in. There is no public page.

## Turning it on

In the team settings (<Kbd keys="o s" />), section **Roadmap**:

- **Roadmap** on adds "Roadmap" under the team in the sidebar. <Kbd keys="g r" /> opens it.
- **Categories:** a label group whose labels are the categories, for example "Characters", "Places",
  "Enemies". A group with single choice works best, so every issue has one category. The order of the
  categories is set right there (or with the arrows in Settings → Labels).
- **Status on the roadmap:** what each status of the team shows as: *Done*, *Testing*, *In progress*,
  *Planned*, *Tentative*, or *Not shown*. Without a choice a status uses the default of its type: completed is
  Done, started is In progress (Testing when the name says review or test), unstarted is Planned, backlog is
  Tentative, triage and canceled are not shown.
- **Export title, subtitle and accent color** for the picture. The logo is the team logo.

## Projects are the stages

Every project of the team is a column of the roadmap and a row of the progress view, in the order of Settings →
Projects (the arrows move a project). Unfold a project there for its roadmap details:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Goal | What the stage is about, a few sentences. Shown under the column. |
| Ready when | When the stage counts as done. |
| Period | A rough time frame, no exact date: a quarter like `2027-Q1` or a month like `2027-03`. |
| Color | The tint of the column and the bar. |
| Status | Planned, active, paused, done or canceled. Canceled projects are left off the roadmap. |

## The roadmap

Each column shows the project's period, its state, "x of y done" with a bar, and the goal. Below it, the
categories with their number of issues; click a category to fold it, or fold all of them with the button at the
top (<Kbd keys="z" />). A card shows the picture, the title, the short text and the stage.

- **Filters** above the columns: categories and stages (several at once). **Only public** shows what the export
  would show. The filters are remembered per team.
- **Opening a card:** in a wide window the issue opens next to the roadmap, fully editable. Click the card again
  or press <Kbd keys="escape" /> to close it, <Kbd keys="enter" /> to open its own page. On a phone the issue
  opens as a page.
- **Keyboard:** <Kbd keys="j" />/<Kbd keys="k" /> within a column, <Kbd keys="arrowleft" />/<Kbd keys="arrowright" />
  to the next column, <Kbd keys="1" /> and <Kbd keys="2" /> switch between roadmap and progress.

## Progress

One row per project, or per category with the switch at the top (<Kbd keys="r" />). The bar runs from the first
issue that was started to the last one completed, or to today while work is open. A dashed outline runs on to
the end of the period; a stage that has not started shows its period dashed. Completed issues are points on the
row. Click a row to unfold its issues, each with its own bar.

Zoom with **Quarter**, **Two quarters** or **Year** (the plus and minus keys). **Today**
(<Kbd keys="n" />) scrolls back to now.

## Public issues

An issue is internal until you mark it as **public**: on the issue, section **Roadmap**, or with the command
"Public on the roadmap". A public issue can have a **public card**:

- a **public title**, if the internal one is not meant for outsiders (otherwise the title is used),
- a **short text** for players, two or three sentences,
- a **cover picture**, picked from the images in the description and comments, or uploaded.

Only these three are ever shown outside the app. The switch goes into the history and can be undone with
<Kbd keys="mod+z" />.

## Exporting a picture

**Export image** (<Kbd keys="shift+x" />) opens a preview. The preview is the file: what you see is what you
download or copy.

- **View:** roadmap or progress. **Format:** 1920 pixels wide (Discord) or 1080 (phone, portrait). **Look:**
  dark or light.
- **Subtitle**, the date ("As of …") and the team logo can be turned off; projects can be left out.
- **Copy** puts the picture on the clipboard, so <Kbd keys="mod+v" /> pastes it into Discord. **Download PNG**
  saves it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Only public issues">
The picture is built from public issues only; the others are left out before anything is drawn, so they do not
count in "x of y done" either. Descriptions, comments, details for agents and identifiers never appear, and the
file carries no text metadata. Project goals are internal text too: they appear only if you check
**Goals of the projects**.
</Aside>

Discord takes files up to 8 MB. If a picture with many cover images is larger, Selfbound draws it smaller until
it fits and says so below the preview.

## For agents

Agents can set up a roadmap through MCP: projects with `create_project` and `update_project`, category labels
with `create_label` and `update_label`, the settings with `update_roadmap`, and the public fields with
`update_issue`. `get_roadmap` shows the result. See [Tools](/agents/tools/#roadmap-projects-and-labels).
