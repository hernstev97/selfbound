Next to its issues, a team can keep a knowledge area: pages for documentation, decisions, research notes and
how-tos. Pages are Markdown, nest into a tree, and link to issues and to each other. Agents can read and write
them too, so what one session found out is there for the next.

<Screenshot name="knowledge" alt="A team's knowledge area: the page tree on the left, a page with title, topics, text and sources on the right" />

## Turn on knowledge for a team

The knowledge area is off for a new team unless you chose **Issues and knowledge** or **Knowledge only** when
you [added it](/concepts/teams/#add-a-team). To turn it on later:

<Steps>
1. Open the team's settings (<Kbd keys="o s" /> for the current team).
2. Under **Knowledge**, choose **Alongside issues**. **Knowledge only** turns the team into a pure knowledge
   team without issues, see [Knowledge-only teams](/concepts/teams/#knowledge-only-teams).
</Steps>

If you press <Kbd keys="g w" /> in a team without a knowledge area, Selfbound says so and offers a shortcut to the
team settings. Once it is on, **Knowledge** appears under the team in the sidebar. You can switch it **Off** again only while the area has no
pages.

## Open it

Press <Kbd keys="g w" /> (**Go to: Knowledge**) to open the knowledge area of the current team, or click
**Knowledge** under the team in the sidebar. The left side shows the team's **Pages** as a tree; the right side
shows what was **Recently changed**, or the page you opened.

Every page has a permanent link that does not change when you move it in the tree. **Copy link** on the page
copies it.

## Pages

Click **New page** to create a page at the top level, or the plus on a row in the tree (**Create subpage**) to
create one below it. A page has:

- **A title.** Click it to rename; <Kbd keys="enter" /> saves.
- **Text** in Markdown, edited like an issue description: click into the text or press <Kbd keys="e" />. Type `/`
  for blocks, paste images with <Kbd keys="mod+v" />. Selfbound saves automatically.
- **A project** (optional), one of the team's [projects](/concepts/projects/). The projects overview counts the
  pages per project and links to them.
- **Topics** (optional), see below.
- **Sources** (optional), see below.
- **Subpages**, listed below the text.

Drag pages in the tree to reorder them: drop on the upper or lower edge of a row to place the page before or
after it, or on the middle to make it a subpage.

### Topics

Topics tag pages, much like [labels](/concepts/labels/) tag issues, but they are separate from labels and belong
to one team. Add a topic below the page title; type a name that doesn't exist yet and choose **Create topic** to
make a new one.

Filter the tree by project or topic with the filters above it (**All projects**, **All topics**). While a filter
is set, the tree becomes a flat list of matching pages.

### Sources

Under **Sources**, record where the knowledge comes from: a **URL** or a title, plus optionally the author or
publisher, the publication date, the date you accessed it, and a note on what the source supports. Sources can
be edited, reordered and removed.

### Revisions

**Revisions** in the page header lists earlier versions of the text. Before the text is overwritten, Selfbound saves
the old version: at most every 10 minutes while one author keeps editing, and always when a different author or
an agent changes it. Open one to preview it, and click
**Restore this revision** to make it the current text again. The text it replaces becomes a revision itself, so
nothing is lost.

## Links between pages and issues

To link to a page from an issue description, a comment or another page, type `/` in the editor and choose **Link
knowledge page**, or type `[[`. Pick the page, and Selfbound inserts a link with its title.

In page text, you can mention issues by their identifier, such as `ACME-42`; it becomes a link.

Selfbound tracks these links in both directions:

- An issue shows a **Knowledge** section with the pages it links to (**linked**) and the pages that mention it
  (**mentions this issue**).
- A page shows where it is **Mentioned in**, and lists its **Mentioned issues**.

Links keep working when an issue moves to another team or a page moves in the tree.

## Search

Search (<Kbd keys="/" />) also finds pages: by title and by text. Page hits appear in their own **Knowledge**
section.

## Trash

**Move to trash** on a page deletes it together with all its subpages, with **Undo** right after. **Deleted** in
the knowledge area lists what is in the trash; **Restore** brings a page back with its subpages. After 30 days,
pages in the trash are deleted for good.

<Aside type="note">
Agents can create pages, add to them and edit them through MCP, but only you can delete and restore pages. See
[Agents](/agents/overview/).
</Aside>
