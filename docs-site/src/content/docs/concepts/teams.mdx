A team is a workspace for one area of your work: a product, a client, a side project. Every issue belongs
to exactly one team, and the team's key is the first part of the issue's identifier. Issue 42 in the team
with the key `ACME` is `ACME-42`.

## What belongs to a team

Each team has:

- **A name and a key.** The key has 2 to 6 characters (capital letters and digits, starting with a letter)
  and appears in every identifier. You can rename it later; old identifiers keep working, see
  [Rename the key](#rename-the-key).
- **A logo and a color.** The logo is an SVG or PNG, square, at most 256 KB. You can add a second logo for
  dark backgrounds. Without a logo, Selfbound shows a placeholder made from the key and the team's color.
- **Its own statuses.** Every team has its own workflow, see [Statuses and workflow](/concepts/workflow/).
- **Its own projects.** A project belongs to exactly one team, see [Projects](/concepts/projects/).
- **Optionally, a knowledge area** with pages for notes and documentation, see [Knowledge](/concepts/knowledge/).

Some things span all teams: [sprints](/concepts/sprints/), workspace [labels](/concepts/labels/), the
[inbox and the triage view](/concepts/triage-and-inbox/), and search.

## Add a team

<Steps>
1. Press <Kbd keys="o n" /> or click the plus next to the teams in the sidebar. The **Add team** dialog opens.
2. Enter a **Name**. Selfbound suggests a **Key** from the first letters of the name; change it if you like.
3. Under **Content**, choose **Issues**, **Issues and knowledge** or **Knowledge only**.
4. Optionally upload a **Logo**.
5. Click **Create team**.
</Steps>

You can also add a team under **Settings → Teams → New team**. A new team starts with eight default
statuses (Triage, Backlog, Todo, In Progress, In Review, Done, Canceled, Duplicate), which you can rename,
extend or archive later.

## The current team

The sidebar shows one team at the top as the **Current team**, with links to its Issues, Backlog, Projects,
Knowledge (if turned on) and Settings, plus Triage while the team has issues waiting there. The other teams
are listed below under **Teams**.

- Press <Kbd keys="o t" /> (**Switch team …**) to pick another team. You stay on the same kind of page where
  that makes sense: from the projects overview you land on the other team's projects, from a knowledge page in
  the other team's knowledge area.
- Press <Kbd keys="o s" /> (**Current team settings**) to open the settings of the current team.

## Team settings

Open a team under **Settings → Teams**, press <Kbd keys="o s" />, or click **Settings** under the current team
in the sidebar. There you change:

- **Name**, **Key** (see [Rename the key](#rename-the-key)) and **Color**.
- **Dormant**: see below.
- **Knowledge**: **Off**, **Alongside issues** or **Knowledge only**.
- **Default model**: the model for the team's issues that set none themselves and whose parent sets none,
  with effort and speed. See [Model](/concepts/issues/#model).
- **Logo** and **Logo for dark backgrounds**.
- **Workflow** and **Fixed order**: the team's statuses, see [Statuses and workflow](/concepts/workflow/).

The order of teams under **Settings → Teams** is the order in the sidebar; use the arrows to move a team up
or down.

## Rename the key

Open the team's settings, click the **Key**, type the new one and press <Kbd keys="enter" />. If the team already
has issues, Selfbound asks first, because every identifier changes: `OPS-24` becomes `ACME-24`. The numbers stay
the same.

The old key keeps working:

- **Links and bookmarks** with an old identifier open the issue, and the address changes to the new identifier.
  Team pages under the old key, such as `/team/OPS/backlog`, lead to the new key.
- **Mentions** in descriptions, comments and knowledge pages show the new key: a mention typed as `OPS-24` reads
  `ACME-24`, with the issue's status. Hover shows the typed form. Only the display changes; the saved text, its
  versions, search and what agents read stay as written. Rename the key back and the mentions read `OPS-24` again.
- **Search, the command menu and agents** find issues under their old identifiers. Agents can also still name
  the team by its old key.
- **Branch names and pull requests** with an old identifier still link to the issue and close it on merge, see
  [GitHub](/integrations/github/).

Once a team has had issues, its old key stays reserved for it, so an old identifier never points to another team's
issue. No other team can take it, but the team itself can switch back to it. The settings list a team's earlier keys under **Key**.

<Aside type="note">
Identifiers outside Selfbound keep the old key: commit messages, branches you already pushed, notes in other
tools. Selfbound still understands them wherever they come back in.
</Aside>

## Dormant teams

Turn on **Dormant** for a team you are not actively working on. A dormant team:

- moves out of the main team list in the sidebar into the collapsible **Inactive** section,
- is left out of [sprint planning](/concepts/sprints/),
- still appears in search, in lists such as **All issues**, and in the team switcher.

Nothing is deleted, and you can turn **Dormant** off again at any time.

## Knowledge-only teams

A team set to **Knowledge only** holds pages instead of issues, for example sources and notes on a topic.
It has no issues and no workflow: it is left out when you create or move issues, accept from triage, set up
recurrences and plan sprints. Opening such a team takes you straight to its knowledge area.

You can switch a team to **Knowledge only** only while it has no open issues and no active recurrences;
completed issues stay. Switching back to **Alongside issues** always works. **Off** is only possible while the
knowledge area has no pages. More in [Knowledge](/concepts/knowledge/).

## Move issues to another team

Press <Kbd keys="mod+shift+m" /> (**Move to team …**) on an issue or a selection and pick the target team.
You can also click the team in the issue's properties.

When an issue moves:

- **It gets a new identifier** in the target team, for example `OPS-24` becomes `ACME-120`. The old
  identifier keeps working: links, search and agents still find the issue under it.
- **Its status** becomes the matching status of the target team: the same built-in status (such as Todo) if
  there is one, otherwise a status with the same name, otherwise one of the same type. The
  [fixed order](/concepts/workflow/#fixed-order) does not apply to moves.
- **Its project is removed**, because a project belongs to exactly one team.
- **Labels of the old team are removed.** Workspace labels stay.
- Everything else stays: priority, estimate, model, due date, sprint, parent, description, comments and
  history. An issue without its own model then inherits the default model of the new team.

If the move would remove a project or labels, Selfbound lists what changes and asks you to confirm first.
Afterwards a message such as "OPS-24 is now ACME-120" appears with **Undo**.

<Aside type="note">
Teams cannot be deleted in the app. If you no longer need one, mark it as dormant.
</Aside>
